# Automation Sales Orders Analytics (Macro & Micro Scopes)

###  Project Overview
The objective is to analyze a comprehensive sales orders dataset (`Automation_sales_orders`) to extract actionable insights. 

To provide a targeted analysis, the project scope is strategically divided into two distinct levels:
1. **Macro Scope (Strategic & Financial):** Focused on high-level revenue generation, customer segmentation, and market performance.
2. **Micro Scope (Logistics & Operations):** Focused on granular operational efficiency, delivery performance, and product return rates.

---

###  Tech Stack & Methodology
* **Data Exploration & Cleaning:** Microsoft Excel (Power Query)
* **Data Modeling & Calculations:** Power Pivot, DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* **Data Visualization & UI/UX:** Power BI & Advanced Excel Dashboards
* **Key Skills Applied:** ETL processes, Relational Data Modeling, Diagnostic Analysis, Data Storytelling.

---

### 🎨 Dashboards & Key Insights

#### 1. Strategic & Financial Performance (Macro Scope)
*Designed to give executives a high-level view of revenue, profitability, and customer distribution.*

#### 2. Logistics Operations & Product Performance (Micro Scope)
*Designed for operational managers to track delivery efficiency, discount impacts, and return behaviors.*
